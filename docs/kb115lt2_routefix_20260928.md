## Rule

The original dispatcher increments its cursor even when a pickup supplies less
wheat than requested. The repair records that particular positive shortfall.
On the next observation it requires the same route, item list, unit, cursor and
shed position, plus the exact expected increase in carried wheat. The checkpoint
is consumed once whether the repair succeeds or rejects. It never retries
`pick_none`, rewinds the cursor, waits for future stock, or adds a market order.

The extra amount is bounded by the original residual and the unmet demand of
existing live FEED targets before the route's next normal wheat pickup. Already
fed, disappeared, replaced and explicitly retiring cohorts are excluded.
Retirement keys with a false value are excluded too. A route segment containing
an ambiguous wheat harvest or delivery is rejected.

All remaining normal wheat pickups across all workers are reserved first.
Earlier workers' issued actions already debit the shared shed ledger. Thus a
later worker's claim cannot be consumed by the retry. The existing
`sd_wheat_pick_now=1` accounting is required, and includes the extra pickup in
the same-hour market reserve.

A conservative bound counts one extra pickup, all remaining route operations
(including FEEDs the broken route would skip), travel, release times and maximum
unresolved pickup/structural waits. It must fit through H23. This may reject
feasible routes; it does not assume that future actions by other workers leave
every shared tile unchanged. Opportunistic fertilizer mode disables this repair.

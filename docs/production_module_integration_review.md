# Production module integration audit

## Scope and baseline

Reviewed `CLAUDE.md`, the first execution experiment, `mg_slots.py`, `tape_calendar.py`, `mgt_sheep.py`, the builder, and the assembled `agents/mgt_m1.py`. The earlier experiment validates command execution for four-plot isolated bundles. It does not validate the requested one-tile wheat-to-carrot overlay on the live sheep-enabled agent; the experiment itself calls out real-tile and future-worker-time reservation as the next gate.

The baseline entry chain is `mgt_kaggle_entry -> agent (sheep wrapper) -> _SHP_PARENT (native tape agent) -> _MGT_IMPL / chassis`. The sheep wrapper hides its extra hands and inventories before calling the tape agent, then merges native commands back into real hand indexes. It may add new hires and market orders after the parent returns. The tape router can switch at day starts from day 3 onward. Tape calendars assume the exact route and tape-native hire schedule.

Available reference opponents include `agents/v50_public.py` (453,739 bytes) and `agents/v48_public.py` (357,742 bytes). Both are large, layered public benchmark agents with many successive wrappers and multiple `agent` definitions; use Kaggle's last-callable loader for validation. They are reasonable live opponents for smoke coverage, but V50 has already been the isolated-module experiment's opponent and does not substitute for a full baseline-vs-overlay comparison. Avoid reading/searching the packed action payloads wholesale.

## Priority integration hazards

1. **Calendar must be based on the exact pinned route.** `tape_calendar.py` simulates positions from the chosen tape, including tape hires and daily respawns. If `_mgt_router` switches while a crop program owns a tile, the predicted visits and actual route diverge. Pin the selected route from program commitment through successful harvest/cleanup or explicit abort. Release only when every active program has ended; verify router state and calendar route agree each call. A router switch on the same step as selection must be handled before building the calendar.

2. **Do not spend or borrow sheep-overlay hands for crop work.** `_shp_hidden` deliberately removes sheep-overlay hands/inventories when invoking the tape. The native calendar's unit index 0 is farmer and indices 1..N are tape hands; real indexes may be shifted by sheep hires. Keep module selection and rewrite in the native layer's hidden/index space, or explicitly map native unit index to real hand index after merge. Never rewrite a worker in `_SHP_CFG`'s own set. New crop visits must not change movement, `HIRE`, or the number/order of tape hands, since this changes spawn positions and tape alignment.

3. **Protect same-day baseline purchases and seed commitments.** The old slot layer's `_mgs_seeds` reserves only its own future crop seeds and trims the replaced crop's tape seed orders against remaining tape plantings. The new WHEAT-to-CARROT path needs the equivalent accounting: reserve CARROT seeds for the committed module; remove/cap only WHEAT seed orders corresponding to replaced future PLANT visits; retain WHEAT seeds for all still-live baseline visits; never count private seed inventory earmarked for another module or any baseline crop. Account for the action's planting before market processing and for the 10-order cap. Also preserve `_shp_feed_guard`'s WHEAT product purchase and animal-feed pickup budget—WHEAT seed accounting and WHEAT product/feed accounting are separate pools.

4. **The crop transition must follow the live board, not planned state alone.** A tape planting visit may encounter a weed, a crop still present, or a tile no longer empty. Commit only if the actual tile is plantable and the next-step seed purchase is feasible. Confirm a requested planting from the next observation. On a missed planting, weed, unexpected disappearance, failed harvest, or no longer reachable harvest, clear reservation/credit state and return control of that tile to baseline. Do not treat a one-time crop's expected yield as delivered until the engine confirms it.

5. **Wheat has economic and care side effects.** Wheat is both a crop and animal feed stock. Replacing a wheat planting may remove future shed replenishment that the tape's animal pickups depend on. Check future same-day feed pickups and reserve sufficient shed wheat, including sheep overlay demand, before converting the tile. The existing `_shp_reserved` and `_shp_feed_guard` protect scheduled pickup quantities; make sure the module layer does not bypass them or sell the reserved stock. Carrot sales must be bounded by module harvest credit so they do not accelerate sales of the tape's own carrots.

6. **Order and wrapper precedence matters.** The sheep wrapper can append feed purchases, sales, and hires after the parent call. Place module command rewrites where they affect native commands before real-hand merging, and apply market edits in a way that respects subsequent guards. Keep original movement commands untouched. The generated file must still end with the fresh `mgt_kaggle_entry` callable, since Kaggle chooses the last new callable name.

## Scheduler / transition checks

`_mgs_plan` is a useful reference but its serviceability proof assumes all usable visits on the tile belong to the overlay and that the route is stable. It requires a planting-day visit, enough watering opportunities, a later harvest visit, and no conflicting baseline plant. For carrots (first yield day 2, max day 3, max yield 4), test both the fast two-day harvest and the watered/fertilized four-unit path, including whether fertilization is in scope. Check harvest timing against within-day visit order: water and harvest must not be inferred from a visit already consumed that step. Preserve `DIG` behavior for a weed on the committed tile and do not reinterpret movement, shed operations, `PLACE`, or animal operations as crop slots.

## Recommended focused validation cases

1. **Exact execution and accounting:** run the actual Kaggle last-callable loader in both seats against V50 on several natural seeds. Use one eligible module at a time. Record each commitment, tile, native unit, route, seed reserve, actual plant confirmation, carrot harvest, sale, and ledger reconciliation. Assert expected output per completed module, no duplicate planting/harvest, and no credited carrots before harvest.
2. **Route pin lifecycle:** choose a seed/world where the router would otherwise switch during a live program. Assert route ID remains fixed through planting, care, harvest, and cleanup; after completion, switching resumes and the rebuilt calendar matches observed positions. Include module cancellation and lost-crop paths to prove the pin always releases.
3. **Hire/index interaction:** exercise a day with baseline tape hires and a day with sheep-overlay hires, including both adjacent to the module visit. Assert movements and hire orders are byte-for-byte equal to the unmodified `mgt_m1` action, and the rewrite lands on the native actor even when real hand indexes have shifted. Confirm overlay workers retain their normal feed/care commands.
4. **Seed reservation and order-room pressure:** test existing CARROT seeds, no CARROT seeds, a baseline WHEAT seed order for a replaced visit, later WHEAT visits, a same-step baseline planting, and an action already at 9/10 market orders. Assert no seed shortfall or stranded excess, no zero-quantity order, baseline crop seed needs remain covered, and the order cap cannot silently drop a required purchase.
5. **Animal-feed conservation:** target a WHEAT replacement before a scheduled sheep feed pickup. Assert reserved shed wheat is not sold or consumed by the module and no tape pickup becomes short; compare animal feed/care/production outputs exactly with baseline where the module's feed obligation is not intentionally changed.
6. **Empty / obstructed transition:** cover empty tile, existing crop, weed present at commitment, weed appearing before planting, missing seed at planting time, crop lost before harvest, and a native route with no later tile visit. Each invalid or failed transition must avoid planting on the wrong tile and release its seed/route reservations cleanly.
7. **No-op and regression control:** when no candidate satisfies the schedule or economics gate, assert the action equals baseline exactly (including movement, hires, market list ordering, and sheep telemetry). Compare full scores against same-seed unmodified `mgt_m1`; do not use the isolated probe's cash delta as a promotion criterion.

## Implementation review checklist

- Module state is keyed by player and reset safely for a new/replayed episode; repeated-step calls do not double-reserve seeds, pin routes, or credit harvests.
- Module selection sees only currently known shops/market state. Do not use future shop draws or test-only environment hooks in production logic.
- Every active module has an explicit end/abort condition and stores its pinned route and native tile/unit provenance.
- Baseline movement and `HIRE` orders are preserved exactly; overlay-created hand commands are not consumed by the crop scheduler.
- Any yield/economic gate includes displaced future wheat production and its feed value, seed cost, harvest/sale timing, and carrot price impact; it does not compare gross carrot revenue against zero.
- All actions remain within engine order limits and pass final-ledger reconciliation.

# Day-6 land handoff: read-only diagnosis and bounded repair design

The immutable `smoke_v1` live-01 run does buy its second quadrant on D6, but does no planting or construction there that day. This is an execution integration defect before any inference about the quality of the semantic quantities.

The D6 proposal requests two strawberries, three wheat, eight pastures with six sheep/two cows, eight hands and one quadrant. Actual cash moves 729 to 8. Verified spending is land1000, three sheep1500, wheat feed593, wheat seeds30, strawberry seeds200, wages54. Revenue is wheat532 and wool2124. All three original sheep are harvested, producing18 wool; only10 wool is sold during D6. The physical ledger has zero PLANT or BUILD commands and127 PASS commands out of208 unit commands. Land is bought at hour5. The later seeds and sheep are real purchases waiting in inventory, not placed assets.

The mechanism is explicit in the frozen KB115LT source. `_plan` (line1277) runs hourly but skips locked structural tiles (line1307) and waits on locked planting sites planned for today's expansion (line1443). `_tier_pre` (line9706) creates the daily route set only at hour0 with one farmer present. `_sd_pre` returns immediately through that fixed-tier branch on later hours. `_tier_override` (line11824) then overwrites the otherwise available hourly actions with those dawn routes, including PASS after the routes finish. Midday market purchases are based on the updated jobs, which explains buying new animals/seeds despite no construction routes for them.

Calling `_tier_pre` again midday is unsafe. It synthesizes hour1/hour2 hired hands and starts its farmer clock at0, regardless of actual current positions. `_tier_core` could in principle accept actual units, but its surrounding pickup/inventory and route specialization assumptions would require a separate implementation and validation.

A smaller ablation preserves the frozen executor source: use its existing rolling dispatcher only after an actual observed quadrant unlock on a day with planned land addition. Keep the fixed tier before the purchase succeeds, switch `sd_tier=0` for the remainder of that day, and restore the original setting at the next dawn. `_sd_build` already starts real units at the current hour with actual positions and inventories; virtual hires are conditional on hour0 and one present unit. `_sd_step` creates a fresh problem when its day changes and can warm-start subsequent hours.

The switch should clear stale `S['assign']`/`S['walk']`, rolling `L['routes']`/`L['walk']`/`L['lastP']`, and set `L['first_of_day']=True`. Keep actual progress/accounting (`done`, `owner`, `owned`, `sold`) intact. Set `sd_plan_once=0` explicitly if a genuinely rolling search is intended. Bound search time, for example `sd_budget0=.7`, `sd_budget=.35`, `sd_step_cap=.8`, then restore those values with `sd_tier` next dawn. These are proposed bounds requiring measured verification, not proven performance settings.

Archive and remove `S['tier']` when switching. Several market paths access this object without checking `CFG['sd_tier']`: books capacity, immediate delivery sales and evening wheat surplus can otherwise use obsolete remaining harvests or delivery credits. The online recipe enables books-cap behavior, so this is material. Retaining its summary in a separate diagnostic field is fine.

The new wrapper can implement this feature off by default, so frozen KB115LT file hashes and the earlier strict tiling benchmark remain unchanged. A paired development ablation should verify construction/planting dates, existing cohort survival, delivery proceeds, overtime and final margin. No new games were executed for this review.

A separate strategy observation: modern100 DSM tapes add600 melons on D0 and395 on D1, and none on D2–18. The144-seat training corpus also has no D6–12 melon additions. A four-melon bridge after D6 would therefore be a new strategic action, not recovery of missing DSM labels. Its320 seed cost and D16–18 harvest compete with early animals and land/crew funding. It should be tested only after the handoff execution defect is addressed.

## Assessment

**Observed:** all six first action differences occur after the step-288 reveal; each case has a saved switch at D12. Every candidate/Y3 diagnostic pair exactly reproduces its recorded own-action hash, both final cash values, and ledger reconciliation. Candidate and Y3 hire-failure lists are empty across the six cases. Differences appear in requested/completed sales and spending and later in live crop/animal mix; rival cash also changes, affecting competitive margin. This is not evidence of a systematic execution failure in these replays.

**Hypothesis:** the regressions are consistent with switch decisions that improve or alter the farm’s trajectory but misestimate downstream relative value against the specific rival trajectory. For example, positive own-cash deltas in r21-v56 and r46-v56 coexist with negative competitive-margin deltas because rival cash rises more. Ledger item and crop-state changes identify where outcomes differed; they do not alone establish which forecast assumption was wrong.

A disappearing crop can reflect normal harvest or other lifecycle changes; the snapshot comparisons do not call those losses. The first action difference may be market-only where physical actions match. The per-case JSON preserves full item deltas and daily snapshots.

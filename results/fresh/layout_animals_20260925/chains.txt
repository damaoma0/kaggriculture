Fertilizer chains of DSM (3000+ leader) hands, stored Kaggle replays only (no engine runs)
====================================================================================================
Source: data/dsm_replays/episode-*-replay.json, 108 episodes, 109 DSM seat-games (episode 111777242 is DSM vs DSM, both seats counted).
Method: results/fresh/layout_animals_20260925/fert_chains.py re-applies the engine rules for PICKUP/DROP/PLACE/
FERTILIZE/COLLECT_FERTILIZER (data/kaggriculture.py::_apply_unit_action) to each recorded pre-step observation +
recorded action, per unit, and tracks each fertilizer unit (token) from its source to its fate. Replays DO contain
each seat's private inventories, so the simulation is checked against the recorded post-step inventory:
  unit-steps verified (non-day-boundary): 761374; fertilizer-count mismatches: 0; position mismatches: 0.
  At the 30 day-boundary steps (hour 23) inventories are auto-dumped, so those steps are simulated, not checked.
Attribution: fertilizer is fungible; each FERTILIZE consumes the most recently acquired token (LIFO). FIFO and
"holdings homogeneous" shares are reported to show how much that choice matters.
Distances: Manhattan; dshed = distance to the nearest shed-access tile (4,4),(5,4),(4,5),(5,5); "shed visit" =
the unit stands on a shed-access tile (spawn counts).

5) WALKING SAVED BY DIRECT CHAINS vs FETCHING THE FERTILIZER AT THE SHED
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  (a) task formula, per chained FERTILIZE: counterfactual = dist(prev position, shed) + dist(shed, crop), where
      prev = where the unit did its previous effective (non-move) op; actual = |prev - crop|.
      n = 21068 (prev op was the COLLECT itself in 26%)
      saving per chained FERTILIZE: mean 7.42 steps (median 8); actual walked prev->crop 1.22
      per game: 1434 steps = 43.4% of all effective moves (3304/game)
      This is an UPPER bound: it charges a separate shed round trip for every single fertilizer.
  (b) one shed detour per trip (a PICKUP of n covers the whole trip; detour inserted before the trip's first
      chained FERTILIZE): trips with chains 15729 (144.3/game, 1.34 chained FERTILIZE per trip)
      saving per trip mean 6.66; per game 961 steps = 29.1% of effective moves
  (c) pick the fertilizer up at the shed visit that STARTED the trip (every direct chain starts from one):
      walking saved = 0 by construction; the chain instead saves 1 PICKUP action per trip (~144 unit-steps/game) and spends 1 COLLECT per fertilizer.
      shed fertilizer stock at that trip-start step: >=1 in 26% of trips, >= the trip's chained count in 24% (n = 15729).

# Leader production response to late shop reveals vs our UMG-old library

Generated by `scripts/analyze_leader_production_response.py`. No games were simulated; all numbers come from parsing recorded replay/tape JSON already on disk.

## Key findings

- DSM: 108 full replays (109 farm-observations, 1 self-play); UMG-old: 584 tapes. DSM tile counts are exact board state; UMG-old cow counts are an upper bound (see caveats).
- YES for sheep, beyond what the season would do anyway: comparing each late YARN_STORE reveal's paired Δ+6 sheep-tile count against the SAME day-window's unconditional baseline (all observations, no reveal conditioning -- controls for any herd-growth trend that just comes with playing longer), DSM is day15: +2.48 vs baseline +0.39, day18: +1.82 vs baseline -0.39, day21: +0.08 vs baseline -1.37 -- consistently +1.92 sheep ABOVE baseline. UMG-old shows the same direction but smaller: day15: +2.00 vs baseline +0.20, day18: +0.45 vs baseline -0.24, day21: +0.04 vs baseline -1.67 -- +1.40 above baseline on average. (Day 24 has no +6d baseline: day 30 is past the 30-day game.)
- NO for strawberry tile count, by the same test: DSM's paired Δ+6 tracks its own seasonal baseline almost exactly at every testable reveal day (day15: -7.36 vs baseline -9.06, day18: -18.63 vs baseline -18.73, day21: -14.62 vs baseline -14.12), as does UMG-old (day15: +0.58 vs baseline +0.27, day18: -13.77 vs baseline -13.64, day21: -16.71 vs baseline -16.82). The pooled (unconditioned-on-day) numbers look dramatic (-13.41 DSM, -10.18 UMG-old) only because strawberry tile count falls in the back half of every game regardless of reveals (a strawberry planted too late cannot mature by day 29) -- that seasonal decline, not a demand response, is what the pooled number mostly shows. Caveat: tile COUNT cannot see a same-tile response (faster replant after harvest, or added fertilizer for more yield per tile), so this specific metric does not rule out a strawberry response through those channels.
- Conditioning on Yarn Stores revealed after day 12 (0 vs 1 vs 2+), DSM's day-24 sheep mean moves 7.1 -> 6.8 -> 9 (n=55/43/11); UMG-old's moves 5.8 -> 6.9 -> 8.0 (n=354/182/48). Day-12 means in the same buckets are the pre-period control.
- Secondary/caveated: pooling the other 5 leader teams' compact tapes, requested SHEEP purchase units were 515 before a Yarn Store reveal vs 1082 on/after it, across 140 tapes with a Yarn reveal. These are requests, not fills.
- DSM labour/land/cash (context, not the main ask): mean 315.1 HIRE requests/game (hands vanish at day end, so this is ~daily re-hiring, not a one-time cost); midday working roster 11.0 (day12) -> 11.0 (day24); land purchase days are ({7: 109, 10: 109}) in EVERY observation -- DSM's opening land schedule looks fixed; vs opponent (non-self-play games only) mean margin 26,089.4, 107W/0T/0L.
- Full per-day, per-product tables (including melon/tomato/wheat/carrot and the day-18 checkpoint) are in analysis.json and the tables below; this bullet list only calls out sheep/strawberry/cow as the task asked to headline.

## Data and verification caveats

- DSM: 108 full replays parsed (1 are DSM-vs-DSM self-play, contributing 2 farm-observations each; the rest contribute 1), giving 109 total farm-observations. Tile counts are EXACT: read directly from the raw engine `farm['tiles']` dict (`kind`, `crop`, `animal` fields), not from any compact encoding.
- UMG-old: 584 tapes from `data/mg_tapes`, all with day-start `boards`. Sheep and goose counts are exact (unambiguous 2-letter codes 'sh'/'go'). **Cow counts are an upper bound**: the tape's compact board encoding reduces both an occupied cow pasture and an empty (animal-less) coop structure to the same code 'co' (see `agents/mgt_m1.py:_mgt_label`, which builds the same style of code and falls back to the structure kind's own first two letters -- COOP -> 'co' -- when no animal is present). This script does not attempt to resolve that collision.
- Secondary signal only, explicitly caveated: 199 compact action tapes across 5 other leader teams (Boey, M&M&P&Q, Unknown Mother-Goose 'current' [a different, closed-loop build -- not our 584-tape UMG-old library], Vadim Vasilenko, DECEM). These tapes record REQUESTED market orders only, not confirmed fills.
- Event-study and conditioning cells are small where noted (n given in every table); treat single-digit-n cells as anecdotal, not estimates.

## 4. Secondary signal: other 5 leader teams' requested SHEEP purchases vs Yarn reveal

BUY_ANIMAL SHEEP entries are requested market orders from compact action-only tapes, not confirmed purchases. The 10-order-per-turn cap and market stock limits can silently drop or shrink a request (documented for our own UMG-old library's over-requesting in CLAUDE.md). Treat only the direction (more requests at/after the reveal) as signal, not the magnitude.

(commit fdc9bb2 state of scripts/fragments/mgt_sheep.py; SHA-256 14f4def11f51...). Write-up: docs/mg_tape_base.md.

What it is
- Public route-replay chassis core + a library of 584 recorded games of her OLD deterministic policy (submissions
  56266758 / 56266899) + a shop router (demand-count distance at checkpoints, board Hamming term 1.0, switches only
  at day starts) + equilibrium opening (one BUY of the net feed wheat on turn 0).
- Feed guard: buys the wheat the shed will be missing one step before the tape's crew picks it up.
- Care top-up: a hand of ours cares for / feeds animals the tape skips when the banked unit pays (engine price
  curve, next production day must be fed); hired after the tape's last hire, hidden from the tape layer.
- Orphan adoption: animals the current tape never touches again after a tape switch are serviced by us.
- Her Yarn sheep rule is present but gated by a day-by-day glut model (declined almost everywhere vs V50).
- Entry point `mgt_kaggle_entry` is the last new callable in the file (Kaggle's loader rule), asserted at build.

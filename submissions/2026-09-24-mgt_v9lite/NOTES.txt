it skips the commit-to-native clone, drops shortlisted tapes with board Hamming > 12, expands only scouts > +350,
expands the runner-up only if the best fails, and admits on 4 shop futures instead of 8. The package builds its
private runtime in a background thread from step 2 and runs one warm rollout at step 240 (off the clock), freezes
the GC heap during a search, and budgets min({288:30, 360:20, 432:20}[step], bank - 8 - 6 x later reveals).

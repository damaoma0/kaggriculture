Midnight dump day

The day side by side

Board: tile state at the start of the selected hour (letter = crop / animal, bold number = units on the tile, f / c = fed / cared today, a dot = watered). At "midnight dump" it shows the board after hour 23, before tonight's production. Circles are units with the goods they carry; lines are their paths so far. Click a unit's row in the strip to highlight it; on the arm's side, the dashed line is that hand's planned route at the selected hour (the arm's plan log).

Unit strip: one row per unit, one cell per hour: H harvest (coloured by product), K collect fertilizer, F feed, C care, W water, Z fertilize, P plant, G dig, B build, U pickup, D drop / place at the shed (green frame), A place an animal, arrows = moves. The blue bar and small number are the load after the hour. The last column is the midnight dump: carried, with a red frame when some of it is deleted.

Dump order (engine `_drop_inventories_to_shed`): the shed first, then the farmer, then hands 1, 2, ... in index order, each unit's goods in its inventory order; everything past 100 is deleted. So the deletions land on the highest-index hands, whoever overloaded the night. Harvest fates split a unit's deleted units onto its latest harvests of that product.

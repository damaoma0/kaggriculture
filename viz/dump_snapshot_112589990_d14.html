Midnight dump snapshot

Board: tiles at the start of the day (letter = crop / animal, number = units held on the tile). Lines are each unit's path up to the selected hour; circles are the units, with the goods they carry (a larger circle = more goods). The chart shows the shed stock and everything carried, hour by hour, against the 100-unit shed cap; the last bar is the midnight dump (farmer first, then hands in index order; everything past 100 is deleted). Mid-day drops are goods put into the shed before hour 23.
